## Context: the page “Reaction or change of state?”

In a change of state the substance stays the same; in a chemical reaction a new one is formed.

### Why

When ice melts, it is still water. When wood burns, no wood is left. Both change a substance, but in very different ways. On this page you learn to tell them apart.

### Rule

- **Change of state**: In a change of state such as melting or evaporating the substance stays the same. Only the state of matter changes.

Ask yourself: is the substance still the same afterwards? If so, it is a change of state. You can usually reverse it: water vapour becomes water again when it cools. If a new substance was formed, it was a chemical reaction.

### Example

**Example: A candle burns**

Both happen at a burning candle. The wax below the flame melts and becomes liquid: that is a change of state, it stays wax. The evaporated wax burns in the flame to other substances, for example carbon dioxide and water vapour: that is a chemical reaction.

### Exercise vorgang-art

- Task: Which of these processes are chemical reactions?
- Answer format: choose all right answers
- Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
  - ✓ Wood burns. – Burning forms new substances, for example carbon dioxide and ash. It is a chemical reaction.
  - ✓ Hydrogen burns to water. – Hydrogen and oxygen become water, a new substance. It is a chemical reaction.
  - ✗ Ice melts. – Melted ice is still water. Only the state changes.
  - ✗ Water evaporates. – Water vapour is still water. It is only a change of state.
- Solution: Wood burns.; Hydrogen burns to water.
- Explanation: When melting and evaporating the substance stays the same, only the state changes. When burning, new substances are formed.

### Exercise kerzenwachs

- Task: Candle wax is warmed in a pot and becomes liquid. Which statement is right?
- Answer format: choose one answer
- Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
  - ✓ It is a change of state; the wax stays wax. – When it cools it becomes solid again. The substance has stayed the same.
  - ✗ It is a chemical reaction because the wax changes. – Only the state has changed. No new substance was formed.
  - ✗ It is a chemical reaction because heat is supplied. – Heat alone does not show a reaction. What counts is whether a new substance is formed.
- Solution: It is a change of state; the wax stays wax.
- Explanation: When melting, the wax stays the same; only the state changes.

### Exercise zucker-braun

- Task: When sugar is heated strongly, it turns brown and smells of caramel. That is a change of state.
- Answer format: choose “true” or “false”
- Solution: The statement is false.
- Explanation: The brown substance has different properties from sugar: colour and smell are new, and it does not turn back into sugar when it cools. It is a chemical reaction.
